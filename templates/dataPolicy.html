How this installation handles your data
Review these details before uploading research materials
Data Collection & Flow

This open-source edition stores uploaded materials and analysis results in one local workspace. It does not use Firebase or send activity records to a cloud analytics service.

AI processing

AI suggestions use the model service configured for this installation. This may be a local Ollama service or a hosted provider. When you use a hosted provider, transcript content is sent to that provider for processing. Check your AI settings before uploading research materials.

Provider data policies

ChromaScribe does not train models itself. The configured model provider determines its own retention and training policies. Review those terms before processing sensitive data.

Storage & Access

Project files are saved on the computer running ChromaScribe, outside its public asset directory. They remain there when you close your browser or restart the application. There are no accounts or sign-in: anyone who can reach this installation can access the same workspace. Run it on your own computer and keep it accessible only to people who should have access to your data.

Retention & Deletion

This application does not automatically delete stored data. You control the workspace folder and any backups on the computer running ChromaScribe. Clearing browser data does not delete project files. Hosted AI providers may retain a separate copy under their own policies.
